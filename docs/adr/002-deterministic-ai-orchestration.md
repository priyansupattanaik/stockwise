# ADR 002: Deterministic AI Tool Orchestration & Provenance

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Standard generative AI tools are prone to hallucinations, fabricating numbers, ratios, or ticker prices. In equity analysis, unverified estimates or invented numbers are dangerous and unacceptable.

## Decision
1. **Tool-Registry Decoupling**: The AI layer communicates exclusively through an immutable tool registry (`AIToolRegistry`) for quotes, filings, screener execution, risk metrics, and backtesting.
2. **Explicit Citations (`SourceCitation`)**: Every numerical assertion in reports must be linked to a machine-readable citation detailing the exact field, statement period, SEC filing accession, or computation timestamp.
3. **Deterministic Backtesting**: Backtesting requests are translated into structured strategy ASTs (`StrategySpec`) and passed to pure code execution in `packages/analytics`, preventing the model from imagining simulated returns.
4. **Informational Non-Advice**: No buy or sell directives are generated by the model.
